Rabbit Cottage is a silly name I chose for our house because 1. it looks sort of like an english cottage and 2. there's a lot of rabbits constantly eating the plants. It could have just as easily been Woodchuck Cottage, or Whistle Pig House, but those don't have the same ring to them. I have made a lot of choices about this house and yard that I want information about to survive me. Especially all the recipes - food and house maintenance, paints, oils, etc. This is a living document of everything I did here for my family - if it's useful to you, too, then all the better!

I made it look like an old book - deal with it!

Underpinning the organization is a Johnny Decimal categorization system - it's kind of arbitrary, but I felt overwhelmed by simply too much information with no way to structure it. The website itself is built with 11ty, but I tried to limit plugins etc / weird formatting etc., just taking advantage of it to render markdown.

The font? It's just your default serif font. Probably Times New Roman.

May the house stand forever, its gardens flush and teeming with bees and pollinators, rabbits taking shelter from the neighborhood cats.
